What we collect

When you request a quote through our website or ads, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, and project details (such as garage size and timeline).

How we use it

We use this information to:

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. Your information stays with Cova Coatings.

By submitting a quote request, you agree we may contact you by phone, text message, or email about your project. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP.

Our site uses standard analytics and advertising pixels (including Meta Pixel) to measure site traffic and ad performance. These tools may collect anonymized browsing data.
